Frequently Asked Questions

Can you convert a storage compartment to a livewell?

Often, yes. We check that the compartment is sound and sits where it can drain overboard, then seal and line it, add an inlet, overflow and drain, and plumb in a pump. If the box is too small or in the wrong spot, we’ll tell you and suggest a better option.

What size livewell is best for my boat?

It depends on what you keep alive and how long you’re out. Rounded corners and a deeper well keep bait healthier, and the size has to suit your deck space and the boat’s balance once it’s full of water. We’ll recommend a size and shape when we see the boat.

Do you install pumps and aerators?

Yes. We install and upgrade recirculating and raw-water pumps, aerators, spray bars, seacocks, valves and plumbing, and we can add a timer or a switch at the helm.

Can you make a custom fish box?

Yes. We build insulated fish boxes to your size, either as a new in-deck box or by converting an existing compartment, with a gasketed lid, a drain and a gelcoat finish matched to your boat.

Will the livewell stay cool in hot weather?

An insulated well with a tight, gasketed lid holds its temperature far better than a bare fiberglass box. Steady circulation with fresh water helps too. We can insulate new builds and many existing wells.

How long does a modification take?

A pump or plumbing upgrade is often done in a day or two. A new livewell or fish box build usually takes a week or more, because the laminate and gelcoat need time to cure. We’ll give you a timeline with your quote.
