What is a Storage
Compartment Repair?

Storage compartments are essential for keeping your boat organized and your gear protected. Over time, they can crack, delaminate, leak, or have hardware failures. We repair and rebuild fiberglass compartments, replace lids and hardware, fix drainage issues, and build custom storage solutions to keep your boat functional and watertight.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you repair a leaking storage compartment?

Yes. Leaks usually come from a worn lid gasket, a cracked or delaminated compartment, a failed drain fitting, or old sealant around the hardware. We find where the water gets in, repair the fiberglass, and re-seal the lid, drain and fittings with marine-grade materials.

Do you replace lids and hardware?

Yes. We replace hinges, latches, gas struts and locks, and repair or rebuild cracked lids. If the original hardware is no longer made, we fit a stainless equivalent and fill and refinish the old holes.

Can you build a custom storage compartment?

Yes. We build in-deck and console compartments from marine-grade fiberglass, sized to your gear, with drainage and a lid finished to match your boat.

How do you ensure the compartment is watertight?

We bed every fitting in marine sealant, fit a new gasket to the lid, make sure the compartment drains properly, and test it for leaks before the job is done.

How long does a storage compartment repair take?

A lid or hardware replacement is often quick. Fiberglass repairs and custom builds take longer, because the laminate and gelcoat need time to cure. We’ll give you a timeline with your quote.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the compartment, the damage, and the hardware involved. Send us a few photos, and we’ll give you a free, no-obligation quote.
