The Importance of Bulkheads

Your Bulkheads Are Part
of the Boat’s Structure.

Bulkheads help divide and reinforce the hull, support loads, and maintain your boat’s shape. Over time, they can be affected by water intrusion, rot, delamination, impact, stress cracks, or failed bonding. Our team repairs and replaces damaged bulkheads with high-quality materials to restore strength and reliability.
