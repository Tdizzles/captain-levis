Custom Consoles & Dash Panels

What is a Custom Console or
Dash Panel?

Custom consoles and dash panels are fiberglass structures designed to house your electronics, gauges, switches, and controls. Whether you’re upgrading an outdated setup or building a completely custom console, we create solutions that are strong, functional, and built to match your boat.

Get Your Custom Console Quote
Popular Custom Console Options

Full Custom Consoles

Complete console builds tailored to your boat and gear.

A full console build starts with how you run the boat: where you stand, what electronics you want and how much storage you need. We design it around your deck layout, fabricate it from marine-grade fiberglass at our Largo yard, then install it, wire in your gear and finish it in gelcoat matched to your boat.

Get a Free Quote

Dash Panel Upgrades

Replace or upgrade existing dash panels for a cleaner setup.

Older dashes tend to collect a patchwork of holes from past electronics, or simply don’t have room for a bigger screen. If the console itself is sound, we build a new panel laid out for the displays and gauges you run today, fit it cleanly, and leave room behind it for wiring and service access.

Get a Free Quote

Integrated Storage

Glove boxes, drawers, and custom compartments.

Most consoles have space that could be holding your phone, keys, tools or tackle. We can add a recessed glove box, drawers or a compartment with a door, either in a new build or cut into your existing console, laminated in fiberglass, fitted with marine hardware and finished to match.

Get a Free Quote

Electronics & Switch Panels

Custom layouts for GPS, sonar, radios, and switches.

Tell us what you run now and what you plan to add, and we’ll lay out the panel around it: chartplotters and displays, sonar, VHF and stereo, gauges, switches and USB or 12V outlets. Everything is placed where you can see and reach it from the helm, with room behind for wiring and service access.

Get a Free Quote

Leaning Posts & Add-Ons

Fiberglass leaning posts, backs, rod holders, and more.

A leaning post or backrest built to match your console gives you a solid place to brace while running and more room for gear. We fabricate them in fiberglass to fit your deck, with rod holders, storage or cooler space built in if you want them, and mount them to reinforced points on the deck.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a custom console build take?

It depends on the job. A dash panel upgrade is often done in a few days. A full custom console usually takes a few weeks, because the laminate and gelcoat need time to cure between stages. We’ll give you a timeline with your quote.

Can you match my boat’s existing color?

Yes. We tint the gelcoat to your boat as it looks today, including any fading, so the new console or panel looks like it came with the boat.

What electronics can you integrate?

Chartplotters and multifunction displays, sonar, VHF and stereo, gauges, switch panels, and USB or 12V outlets. Tell us what you run now and what you plan to add, and we’ll lay out the panel with room for wiring and service access.

Do you offer mobile service?

Yes. Measuring, dash panel swaps and many installs can be done at your dock or driveway anywhere in Tampa Bay. Full console builds are fabricated at our yard in Largo, then installed on your boat.

Can you work with my existing console?

Often, yes. If the structure is sound, we can cut in a new dash panel, add storage or a glove box, extend the console, or refinish it instead of building a new one from scratch.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the size, the layout and the electronics involved. Send us a few photos and what you have in mind, and we’ll give you a free, no-obligation quote.
