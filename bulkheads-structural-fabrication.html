Bulkheads & Structural Fabrication

What Are Bulkheads
and Why Do They Matter?

Bulkheads are vertical structural walls inside your boat that provide
strength, separate compartments, and support key components
like the deck, console, and engine. Damaged or rotten bulkheads can
lead to soft floors, loose consoles, and serious structural issues.
We replace and fabricate bulkheads using marine-grade materials
to ensure a strong, long-lasting repair.

Common Bulkhead & Structural Issues

Rotten Wood Bulkheads

Water intrusion leads to soft, rotted, or failing bulkheads.

Cracked or Delaminated

Stress, impact, or age can cause cracks and separation.

Loose Center Console

A failing bulkhead can cause your console to move or flex.

Soft or Spongy Floors

Often a sign of damaged bulkheads or stringers.

Transom Support Issues

Weak or damaged bulkheads can affect engine mounting and performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my bulkheads are bad?

Common signs are a soft or spongy deck, a console or leaning post that moves when you push on it, cracks where the bulkhead meets the hull or deck, and dark, wet or crumbling wood inside a hatch. If you notice any of these, we can inspect the structure and tell you what we find.

What materials do you use for new bulkheads?

Marine-grade plywood or a rot-proof composite core, laminated with fiberglass and epoxy or vinylester resin, then tabbed to the hull. We’ll recommend the right core for your boat and how it’s used.

Can you replace bulkheads in a center console?

Yes. We often replace the bulkheads that support the console, removing it when we need to, then reinstall and re-bed it on the new structure so it’s solid again.

How long does a bulkhead repair take?

It depends on how much structure is involved. A single bulkhead is often a few days; replacing several, or bulkheads tied into stringers and the deck, can take a few weeks because the laminate needs time to cure. We’ll give you a timeline with your quote.

Will the repair be stronger than the original?

In most cases, yes. Many factory bulkheads are lightly glassed wood. Ours are built with marine-grade or composite cores and fully tabbed and sealed, so water can’t get in and start the rot again.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on how many bulkheads need work, how hard they are to reach, and whether the deck or console has to come up. Send us a few photos and we’ll give you a free, no-obligation quote.
