Frequently Asked Questions

How much does edge and rub rail damage repair cost?

It depends on how long the damaged run is, how deep the chips go and whether the edge profile needs rebuilding. Send us a few photos through the form below or call 727-391-7639 for a free quote.

Will the repair be visible?

Our goal is that you can’t find it. We rebuild the edge to its original shape, then color match, wet sand and polish the repair so it blends into the surrounding gelcoat.

Can you match my boat’s existing gelcoat color?

Yes. We tint the gelcoat to your hull as it looks today, including any fading, so the repair matches the boat rather than the original factory chart.

How long does an edge repair take?

Small chips along an edge are often finished in a day. Longer runs, or edges that need the profile rebuilt, take longer to allow for cure time — we give you a timeline with the quote.

Can you repair rub rail damage below the waterline?

Yes, but the boat has to be out of the water so the repair can dry and cure. We can work on your trailer or lift, or haul the boat at our Largo boat yard.
