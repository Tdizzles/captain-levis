Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I detail my boat?

In Florida’s sun and salt, most boats kept in the water do best with a wash every few weeks and a compound, polish and wax once or twice a year. Boats on a lift or under cover can usually go longer. We can suggest a schedule once we have seen your finish.

What’s included in a full boat detail?

A full detail covers the outside and the inside: a thorough wash, compounding and polishing to remove oxidation, marine UV wax, non-skid and deck cleaning, and cleaning and conditioning of seats, cushions and interior surfaces.

Can you remove heavy oxidation?

Yes. We cut chalky, oxidized gelcoat back with professional-grade compounds and polish it to a gloss. If the gelcoat is too far gone for a detail to hold, we will tell you and talk through oxidation removal or restoration instead.

Do you come to marinas or boat yards?

Yes. Our mobile crew comes to slips, lifts, marinas and driveways around Tampa Bay, or you can bring the boat to our shop and boat yard in Largo.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the boat’s size and condition. A wash and wax on a smaller boat is often done in a day, while a full compound and polish on a larger or heavily oxidized boat can take longer. We will give you a time frame with your quote.

Do you use marine-safe products?

Yes. We use premium, professional-grade marine compounds, polishes and UV-protective waxes made for gelcoat and the waters our customers boat in.
