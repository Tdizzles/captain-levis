Our Detailing Process

Wash. Compound. Polish. Protect.

Every detail follows the same order, because each stage only works if the one before it was done properly.

  1. 1

    Wash & Assess

    A thorough clean first, then we check how far the oxidation and staining go.

  2. 2

    Compound

    Professional-grade compounds cut back the weathered gelcoat to a fresh surface.

  3. 3

    Polish with AQUABUFF

    Oxidation removed and gloss restored on hulls, topsides and brightwork.

  4. 4

    Wax & Protect

    Marine UV wax seals the finish; non-skid and interiors are cleaned to match.

Why Detail Your Boat

The Benefits Go Beyond the Shine

A regular detail is the cheapest protection a fiberglass boat gets. Here is what it does for you.

Restored Shine & Luster

Hulls and topsides brought back to the depth of gloss they had when the gelcoat was new.

Protection From Salt, UV & Pollution

A sealed, waxed surface repels the things that chalk gelcoat and pit metal in Florida.

Easier Ongoing Maintenance

Dirt, salt and scum rinse off a protected finish instead of bonding to it, so every wash is quicker.

Added Value When You Sell

A detailed boat shows that it has been cared for, which matters when it is time to sell or trade.
