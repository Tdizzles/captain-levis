## 2. Redesigned site — what exists

- **Pages (45):** homepage + 9 top-level service pages + ~35 sub-pages (gelcoat chips/scratches/cracks, hull, deck,
  stringer, transom, bulkhead, custom fabrication family). Design system is `styles.css` `:root`; each page has its
  own prefixed stylesheet. Recipe in `BUILDING-SERVICE-PAGES.md`.
- **Shared blocks** copied byte-for-byte into all 45 pages: header/nav, footer, contact form `#quote`, chat widget.
  Any change to those blocks is a 45-file edit. A shared-include/build step is needed before the chatbot, nav,
  text-size controls, consent banner and legal links can be changed once.
- **Navigation:** desktop dropdown + stacked mobile accordion (`script.js`). Top-level links Boat Yard, Detailing,
  About, Reviews, and the logo are `href="#"`. Footer Privacy/Terms/Sitemap are `href="#"`. Social links are `#`.
- **Broken links:** `detailing.html` and `boat-yard.html` are linked from all 45 pages and do not exist;
  `services.html` (footer) does not exist. No about, contact, privacy, terms or cookie pages.
- **Contact form:** `#contact-form` on every page. `script.js` prevents submit, checks HTML validity, disables the
  fields and shows the success message. **Nothing is sent anywhere.**
- **CAPTCHA:** the "I'm not a robot" box is a styled checkbox (`.robot-check`) with a reCAPTCHA-lookalike badge.
  There is no reCAPTCHA/hCaptcha script, no site key, no token, no server. There is no integration to "fix"; it
  must be built. (The live Strikingly form does load hCaptcha/reCAPTCHA, but that is Strikingly's form, not ours.)
- **Chatbot:** client-only. `replyFor()` in `script.js` is ~15 regexes returning canned strings behind a fake
  typing delay. Chips: "Get a quote", "Services", "Hours & location". Per-page greeting text varies (5+ variants).
  The canned hours reply says **Mon–Sat 8:00 AM–5:00 PM**; the live site says **Mon–Sat 7:30am–3:30pm**.
- **Business facts in the new site vs. verified sources:**
  - Email: new footer uses `info@captainlevis.com` (90 occurrences); live site, blank template and Rev5 PDF all use
    `captainlevis@yahoo.com` (2 occurrences in the new site).
  - Experience: new site says "50+ Years" (109 occurrences); live pages say "fifty years", "47 years", "46 years",
    "40+ years" and "43 years" in different places.
  - Address 11600 66th St N, Largo FL 33773 and phone 727-391-7639 match everywhere. Two legacy pages still say
    the office is in "Bay Pines Industrial Park, St. Petersburg" (stale).
- **SEO:** every page has a `<title>`, meta description and one `<h1>`. No canonical, no Open Graph, no JSON-LD
  (LocalBusiness), no `robots.txt`, no `sitemap.xml`, no `404.html`, no breadcrumbs. GitHub Pages cannot serve 301s.
- **Accessibility:** `lang` and viewport on all pages; no `<main>` landmark; no skip link; 4 `:focus-visible` rules;
  one `prefers-reduced-motion` block. Font sizes are 115× `px` vs 16× `clamp()` → a text-size control needs a
  root-scale strategy (px→rem migration or a documented multiplier), not just `html{font-size}`.
- **Responsive:** breakpoints at 1024/900/820/640/620/480/400/379 exist. Not yet audited page-by-page on devices
  (Phase 2 task). Known family-level bugs from earlier sessions: trust strip touching the screen edge on phones in
  the sbc/ccd lineage; `<ol>` step lists showing stray numbers on many pages.
- **Performance:** `assets/` = 713 files, 17 MB. `captain-chat.png` is 1.2 MB at 1254×1254 and is loaded twice on
  every page (header avatar + chat toggle). No WebP/AVIF, no responsive `srcset`.
- **Analytics/cookies:** none. The new site sets no cookies → a consent banner only becomes necessary once
  analytics, chat persistence or CAPTCHA are added.

no inquiry intake, no remote approval links, no email, no server-side totals validation, no protected audit trail.

## 5. Blockers and decisions for the owner

1. **Hosting for the backend.** GitHub Pages cannot run server code, hold secrets, or issue 301 redirects. The
   marketing site must move to Vercel (same account as the estimater) or equivalent. Recommended: Vercel.
2. **Provider choices** (each needs an account/key): Postgres (Neon via Vercel Marketplace, or Supabase), email
   (Resend, with DNS records on captainlevis.com for a sending address), CAPTCHA (Cloudflare Turnstile recommended;
   hCaptcha acceptable), uploads (Vercel Blob), AI (Anthropic API key with a monthly budget cap).
3. **DNS cutover** of captainlevis.com from Strikingly, and cancelling Strikingly/LiveChat when ready.
4. **Admin dashboard home:** extend the estimater (recommended) rather than building a second admin UI.
5. **Business facts to confirm:** hours, public email, "years" claim, outside storage, nationwide materials
   catalog, fuel-tank replacement as a service, 500-shade gelcoat claim, PWC page consolidation, Resources removal.
6. **Chatbot screenshot** referenced by the brief was not found in `~/Downloads` or in the conversation. It is
   needed for the text-size control placement; the current `.chat-widget` markup is the fallback reference.
7. **Google Search Console / GA4 access** to see which legacy URLs get traffic before any page is removed.
8. **Staff user list** (emails) for admin authentication.
9. **Tax rule** 7% = 6% FL + 1% Pinellas is stated on Rev5; the estimater still flags it unconfirmed.
