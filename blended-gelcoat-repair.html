Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a blended gelcoat repair cost?

It depends on the size of the damage, where it is on the boat and how hard the color is to match. Most blended repairs cost far less than a full respray. Send us a photo or book an inspection and we’ll give you a firm quote.

Will the repair be visible?

The goal is that it isn’t. We match the color, then feather the new gelcoat into the surrounding finish and wet sand and polish it to the same gloss and texture, so the edge of the repair disappears.

How long does a blended repair take?

Most small blended repairs are finished in one to two days, allowing for cure time. Larger areas or multiple repairs take longer — we give you a timeline with the quote.

Can you match my boat’s exact gelcoat color?

Yes. We tint the gelcoat to your hull, not to the factory color code, because sun and age shift the original shade. We test the match on the boat before we blend it in.

Do you offer blended repairs for decks and consoles?

Yes. We blend repairs on hulls, decks, consoles, transoms, hatches and most other gelcoat surfaces. Non-skid areas can be repaired and re-textured to match as well.
