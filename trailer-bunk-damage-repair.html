Understanding Trailer Bunk Damage

What is Trailer Bunk Damage?

Trailer bunks are padded supports that hold your boat during trailering. Over time, contact with the bunks can cause scratches, scuffs, and deeper gouges in the gelcoat and fiberglass — especially during loading, unloading, or long-distance travel. These marks are common, but if left unrepaired they can allow water intrusion, lead to oxidation, and reduce the value and appearance of your boat.

Common Trailer Bunk Damage Examples

Straight Bunk Scratches

Long, straight scratches from carpeted or plastic bunks.

Deep Bunk Gouges

Deeper damage caused by improper loading or worn bunks.

Widespread Scuffing

Repeated trailering can leave multiple scuffs across the hull.

Waterline Bunk Wear

Bunk contact near the waterline can cause significant gelcoat wear.

Edge & Corner Damage

Contact during loading can damage edges and corners.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you repair trailer bunk damage at my dock?

Often, yes — our mobile service comes to your dock, driveway or storage yard. Bunk damage sits low on the hull, so the boat needs to be out of the water with the damaged area reachable. Send us a few photos and we’ll tell you what setup works, or we can do the repair at our yard in Largo.

Will the repair be color matched?

Yes. We tint the gelcoat to your hull as it looks today, including any fading, then blend and polish the repair so it disappears into the surrounding finish.

How long does a trailer bunk repair take?

Light scratches and scuffs are often finished in a day. Deeper gouges that need the fiberglass rebuilt take longer to allow for cure time — we give you a timeline with the quote.

Can you repair deep gouges from trailer bunks?

Yes. We grind out the damaged gelcoat and any loose fiberglass, rebuild the area with marine-grade laminate and filler, then gelcoat, color match and polish it. We’ll also point out worn bunk carpet or misadjusted bunks, so the damage doesn’t come straight back.
