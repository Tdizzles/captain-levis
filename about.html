Our Story

Generations of Boating
Experience

Captain Levi’s has been building custom fiberglass boats, maintaining and repairing fiberglass boats and fishing boats for the last fifty years on the west coast of Florida, Home Port World Fishing Famous John’s Pass.

Our family has dedicated our lives to the water — from boat building and offshore fishing to expert fiberglass repair and detailing. Every project we take on is backed by real-world experience and a deep understanding of what it takes to keep a boat safe, strong, and looking its best.

Meet Our Leader

Captain David Holley
General Manager & CEO

Captain David Holley has been in the boating and offshore fishing business for over forty-three years. He has worked for some of the largest fiberglass yacht builders in the world in every capacity, including Plant Manager.

In the early 1980’s, Captain Holley broke away from the manufacturing business to pursue his love for deep sea fishing and adventure. After crafting a thirty-six foot custom commercial fishing trawler, he spent ten years fishing the offshore waters of the Gulf of Mexico in pursuit of American Red Snapper, Grouper, and Greater Amberjack.

His experience on the sea made him understand more than ever the importance of a safe, well-built vessel. Captain Dave has seen some rough waters — including Class 1 hurricane winds two hundred miles offshore. He has lost good friends to the sea whose boats didn’t come home, and that’s why he and his crew are committed to quality repairs and materials that keep you and your family safe.

The Next Generation

Levi Holley
Now Stone Crabbing

Captain Dave’s son, Levi, was born in 1990 and, like his father, grew up in the boating and fishing business. He spent years at the shop running the Boat Detailing Division and learning fiberglass repair and boat building.

But Levi wanted to go stone crabbing. On the first day of the season, his boat brought in more than 800 lbs of stone crab. After all, he is the son of a son of a son of a son of a sailor man.
