Our Story

From the Factory Floor
to the Gulf of Mexico

Captain Levi’s began as a small garage operation run on a love of boats and the sea. Careful work and word of mouth grew it into the Tampa Bay shop boaters call when the damage is more than skin deep. Today we repair, restore and detail fiberglass boats at our shop and boat yard in Largo, and bring the same crew to your dock or storage yard anywhere in Pinellas County and the Tampa Bay area.

Our general manager and CEO, Captain David Holley, spent the first part of his career with some of the largest fiberglass yacht builders in the world, working in every capacity on the floor and finishing as plant manager. In the early 1980s he left manufacturing to go to sea. He built Pelagia Two, a 36-foot custom commercial fishing trawler, and fished her offshore in the Gulf of Mexico for ten years for American red snapper, grouper and greater amberjack.

Those years shape how we work today. Pelagia Two rode out gale-force winds and hurricane-strength weather two hundred miles offshore, and not every boat Captain Dave knew came home from those storms. Once you have trusted your life to a hull, structural soundness stops being a selling point and becomes the whole job. That is why this shop cares so much about what is under the gelcoat: sound laminate, dry core, and stringers, bulkheads and transoms that are properly bonded.

Captain Dave’s son, Levi, was born in 1990 and, like his father, grew up in the boating and fishing business. He leads our Detailing division, runs this website and keeps learning the craft of fiberglass repair and the materials behind it. When he is not at the shop, you will find him fishing offshore.

Why Boaters Choose Us

in Rough Water

Quality workmanship and quality materials are the whole business. We take on the difficult, specific requests of the people who go to sea, because we have been those people.

Quality Materials

We use the same type of fiberglass materials as the original construction, or current composite materials, laid up to a proper laminating schedule.

Factory-Matching Methods

Repairs follow the techniques the builder used, so the finished work meets or exceeds the original construction standard instead of just covering the damage.
