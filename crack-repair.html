Our Crack & Spider Crack Repair Services

Professional Repairs for All Types of Fiberglass Cracks.

Cracks and spider cracks can compromise your boat’s strength and allow water intrusion. We repair cosmetic and structural cracks using proven marine-grade materials and techniques.

Spider Crack Repair

Repair surface spider cracks and stress fractures to restore a smooth, like-new finish.

Spider cracks are the web of fine lines that spreads out from a fitting, a corner or an old impact, usually because something underneath is flexing. We find the cause first, then grind the cracks out, reinforce the area if it needs it, and fair and gelcoat the repair to match so the cracks don’t come straight back.

Why Repair Fiberglass Cracks?

Small Cracks Can Lead
to Big Problems.

Even small cracks allow water to penetrate the fiberglass, which can cause delamination, soft spots, structural damage, and costly repairs. Repairing cracks early keeps your boat strong, safe, and looking its best.

  • Prevents Water IntrusionKeeps core and laminates dry.
  • Restores StrengthRebuilds the structural integrity of your boat.
  • Stops Further DamagePrevents cracks from spreading due to stress, vibration and UV exposure.
  • Saves MoneyEarly repairs prevent bigger, more expensive repairs later.
  • Looks Like NewProfessional fairing and finishing for a seamless repair.
