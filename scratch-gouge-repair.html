Our Scratch & Gouge Repair Services

Common Scratch & Gouge Repairs

Scratches and gouges not only affect the appearance of your boat, they can also expose the fiberglass to UV rays and water. We repair all types of gelcoat damage, from light surface scratches to deep gouges and impact marks.

Surface Scratches

Remove light scratches, scuffs and marks from everyday use, fenders and dock rash.

Why Repair Scratches & Gouges?

Protect Your Boat
and Prevent Bigger Problems.

Even small scratches can let in water, lead to blistering or delamination over time, and reduce the value of your boat. Professional gelcoat repair keeps your boat looking great and protects the fiberglass underneath.

    Water Intrusion    Keeps moisture out of the laminate.
