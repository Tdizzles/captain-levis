Why Repair Gelcoat Chips?

Stop Small Damage
Before It Grows.

Gelcoat chips expose the fiberglass underneath, which can lead to water intrusion, delamination, and costly structural repairs. A professional chip repair restores your boat’s finish and keeps it protected for years to come.

  • Prevents
    Water Intrusion    Keeps moisture out of the laminate.
  • Stops UV DamageRestores a protective finish that blocks sun and oxidation.
  • Maintains ValueKeeps your boat looking its best and helps resale value.
  • Looks Like NewExpert color matching for a seamless repair.
Our Gelcoat Chip Repair Process

Built Right. Step by Step.

We use professional techniques and marine-grade materials to ensure a durable, invisible repair.

  1. 1

    Inspect & Prep

    Assess the damage, clean the area, and sand for proper adhesion.

  2. 2

    Fill & Shape

    Fill the chip with high-quality gelcoat and shape to match the surrounding surface.

  3. 3

    Color Match

    We precisely match the gelcoat color for a seamless blend.

  4. 4

    Finish & Polish

    Wet sand, polish, and blend for a smooth, glossy, like-new finish.
