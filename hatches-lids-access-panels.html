Hatches, Lids & Access Panels

What is a Hatch, Lid or
Access Panel Repair?

Hatches, lids, and access panels allow you to reach essential components like storage, batteries, fuel systems, and plumbing. Over time, they can crack, leak, become loose, or suffer delamination. We repair damaged fiberglass, replace hardware, rebuild lids, or fabricate new custom panels to ensure a proper fit, watertight seal, and long-lasting durability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you repair a cracked hatch lid?

Usually, yes. We grind out the crack, rebuild the laminate with new fiberglass, and refinish the lid in color-matched gelcoat. If the lid is badly delaminated or the core is rotten, we’ll tell you whether a new lid will cost less than the repair.

Do you replace hinges, latches, and gas struts?

Yes. We replace worn or corroded hinges, latches, locks, pulls and gas struts with marine-grade stainless hardware, and repair stripped or cracked mounting points so the new hardware stays tight.

Can you match my existing hatch or make a custom one?

Both. We can rebuild your existing lid, fit a new OEM-style hatch, or fabricate a custom fiberglass lid or access panel to fit the opening, finished to match your boat’s gelcoat and non-skid.

Will the repair be watertight?

That’s the goal of every job. We check how the lid sits in its frame, fit new gaskets and seals, adjust the hardware so the lid closes evenly, and check for leaks before we hand the boat back.

How long does a hatch repair take?

A hardware or gasket replacement is often done in a day. Fiberglass repairs and new custom lids take longer, because the laminate and gelcoat need time to cure between stages. We’ll give you a timeline with your quote.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the hatch, the damage, and whether we repair or replace it. Send us a few photos and we’ll give you a free, no-obligation quote.
