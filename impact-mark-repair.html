Why Impact Damage Should Be Repaired

Small Impacts Can Lead
to Major Problems.

Even minor collisions can break the gelcoat and underlying fiberglass, allowing water intrusion and causing hidden damage. Repairing impact marks early helps prevent costly structural repairs and keeps your boat safe, strong, and looking its best.

  • Water Intrusion Cracks from impacts can allow water into the laminate and core.
  • Hidden Damage Impact damage may extend deeper than it appears.
  • Structural Weakening Compromised fiberglass can reduce strength over time.
  • Higher Repair Costs Early repairs are faster, more affordable, and prevent major rebuilds later.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does impact mark repair cost?

It depends on how deep the impact goes. A surface ding is a small gelcoat repair; damage that reaches the laminate needs grinding out and rebuilding. We look at it first and give you a free quote before any work starts.

Can you match my boat’s exact gelcoat color?

Yes. We tint the gelcoat to your hull, including faded and older finishes, then blend, wet sand and polish the repair into the surrounding surface.

Will the repair be visible?

Our goal is a repair you can’t pick out from a normal viewing distance. Color matching and blending is how we get there, and we’ll tell you up front if a finish is hard to match.

How long does an impact repair take?

Small cosmetic impacts are often quick. When laminate has to be rebuilt, resin and gelcoat need time to cure between steps, so it takes longer. You’ll get a timeline with your quote.

Can you repair collision damage on my dock?

Smaller impact repairs can often be done at your dock or lift with our mobile service. Holes and structural damage are best handled at our Largo yard.
