# Florida points that affect an estimate (SEED — general practice, not legal advice; the owner's attorney and
# accountant confirm the specifics)

- Written estimate: give the customer a written estimate before work starts and get a signed approval. Our document
  has the owner signature line for that; for claims it is the Owner / Insured line plus the Direction to Pay.
- Changes mid-job: hidden damage or extra work is quoted as a new revision or a separate estimate and approved in
  writing (email is fine) before the work is done. The terms block says this; do not remove it.
- Sales tax: the shop currently charges 7% on the total repair charge (6% state + 1% Pinellas County surtax). Keep
  the tax note on the document. Whether labor on repairs is taxable in a given case is for the accountant; the
  assistant does not change the rule.
- Storage and yard fees: state them per day or per week on their own lines so a customer never sees a surprise.
- Release of the boat: the terms say the boat is released on final payment. Do not add lien language; if a staff
  member asks about liens, say that is for the owner's attorney.
- Customer data: keep only what the estimate needs (name, contact, address, vessel identifiers). Do not copy a
  policy number, SSN, or card details into the document.
