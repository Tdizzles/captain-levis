Our Dock & Trailer Damage Repair Services

Professional Repairs for Real-World Damage.

Dock and trailer damage is one of the most common types of fiberglass repair. We restore scrapes, gouges, cracks, and impact marks caused by docks, pilings, trailer bunks, and loading — using professional marine-grade materials and expert color matching.

Why Repair Dock & Trailer Damage?

Stop Small Damage
Before It Gets Worse.

Scrapes and gouges aren’t just cosmetic. They can expose the fiberglass underneath, allowing water intrusion, UV damage, and more expensive structural repairs over time. A professional repair restores your boat’s appearance and protects it for years to come.

  • Prevents Water IntrusionKeeps moisture out of the laminate.
  • Stops Further DamageSeals and reinforces damaged areas.
  • Maintains ValueKeeps your boat looking its best.
  • Looks Like NewProfessional color matching for a seamless repair.
