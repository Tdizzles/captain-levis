What Are Engine & Rigging
Access Modifications?

Engine and rigging access modifications involve cutting, reinforcing, and fabricating custom hatches, panels, or lids to improve access to your engines, rigging, fuel systems, and other components.
We create solutions that make routine maintenance easier,
whether you need a larger hatch, a new access panel, or a complete custom setup tailored to your boat.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you add a larger engine hatch to my boat?

In most cases, yes. We check what’s under the deck first (stringers, fuel and water lines, wiring), then cut the larger opening, reinforce its edges, and build a matching hatch lid to fit it.

Will the modification affect the strength of my deck?

Not when it’s done right. We frame the new opening with fiberglass and, where needed, extra core or stringer support, so the deck around the hatch is as strong as it was before the cut.

What type of hardware do you use?

Marine-grade stainless hinges, latches, pulls and gas struts, plus gaskets and drain channels where a hatch needs to stay dry. We size the struts to the weight of each lid so it opens easily and stays up.

Can you match my boat’s gelcoat and style?

Yes. We tint the gelcoat to your boat as it looks today and finish the hatch edges, lids and any non-skid to blend in with the rest of the deck.

How long does an access modification take?

A new access panel is often done in a few days. A larger engine hatch or a full custom layout usually takes one to a few weeks, because the laminate and gelcoat need time to cure between stages. We’ll give you a timeline with your quote.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the opening, the reinforcement it needs and the hardware you choose. Send us a few photos and we’ll give you a free, no-obligation quote.
