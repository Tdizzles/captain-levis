Why Boats Oxidize and Fade

Florida’s Environment Takes a Toll.

Constant UV exposure, saltwater, heat, and weather cause gelcoat to oxidize over time. This breaks down the surface, leaving a chalky, faded appearance that can make your boat look old and neglected.

  • UV ExposureBreaks down gelcoat and causes fading.
  • SaltwaterSpeeds up oxidation and surface damage.
  • Heat & WeatherExpands, contracts, and wears down finish.
  • TimeYears of use lead to chalking and discoloration.
