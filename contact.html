Before You Send

What to include

The more of this you can give us up front, the faster we can give you a real answer.

  • Make, model and year of the boat, jet ski or kayak.
  • Where the boat is: on a trailer, in a slip, at a marina or storage yard.
  • What happened: a collision, a soft spot, a leak, fading, or something you cannot quite place.
  • Photos of the damage: one wide shot to show where it is, and a few close-ups.
  • Whether it is an insurance claim, and the claim number if you have one.
Insurance claims welcome.

Tell us it is a claim and we will put together the written estimate and photos your adjuster needs.

Good to Know

Quick answers

Do you come to me, or do I bring the boat to you?

Both. We have a shop and boat yard in Largo, and we run mobile repair and detailing across Pinellas County and the Tampa Bay area. Smaller cosmetic work and detailing are often done at your dock; larger structural jobs are usually better at the yard. We will tell you which makes sense for your boat.

How long does it take to get a quote?

We get back to you as soon as we can, and usually quickly. Clear photos speed things up. Some jobs, especially anything structural, need an in-person look before we can put a number on them, and we will say so rather than guess.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. There is no charge to send us details, talk it over, or have us look at the boat and write up an estimate.

What if I am not sure what is wrong?

That is fine. Describe what you see or feel: a soft spot underfoot, a crack, water where it should not be, a dull finish. Send photos and we will help you work out what it is.
