Our Repair Process

Our Repair Process

From structural rebuilds to finish work, we follow the same proven steps for strong, long-lasting fiberglass repairs.

  1. 1

    Inspect and grind

    We inspect the damage and grind back to sound laminate.

  2. 2

    Rebuild the structure

    Biaxial fabrics, roving, mat and marine cores, laid up to schedule.

  3. 3

    Bond with the right resin

    Epoxy or vinyl ester resin, chosen for strength and adhesion.

  4. 4

    Vacuum bag or infuse when needed

    Full fiber saturation and a dense, void-free laminate.

  5. 5

    Fair the surface

    We fair and shape until the repair follows the boat’s true lines.

  6. 6

    Spray the finish

    Color-matched gelcoat or marine polyurethane.

  7. 7

    Wet sand, long-board and buff

    Finished with AQUABUFF compounds for a deep, lasting shine.
