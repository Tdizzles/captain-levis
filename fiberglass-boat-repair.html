Fiberglass Boat Repair

Fixed Right, From the
First Grind to the Final Buff

Fiberglass hulls, decks, transoms, stringers and bulkheads live under constant stress from water, vibration and sun. Over time gelcoat cracks, laminate delaminates and structural cores rot. Left alone, those problems get worse and start to compromise safety.

At Captain Levi’s, Captain Dave and Levi work with an experienced crew to repair fiberglass boats using factory-matched materials and techniques that meet or exceed the original construction standard. The goal of every repair is the same: restore the original lines, keep the water out, and bring back the strength the boat was built with.

Fishing boats, sport yachts, center consoles, offshore racers, jet skis and kayaks: if it is fiberglass, we can repair it, at our yard or at your dock.

Our Repair Process

The same steps, whether it is a chip in the gelcoat or a new transom.

  1. 1Inspect and grind. Precise damage inspection, then grinding back to sound laminate so nothing compromised stays in the repair.
  2. 2Rebuild the structure. Biaxial fabrics, woven roving, mat and core materials, laid up to a proper laminating schedule.
  3. 3Bond with the right resin. Epoxy and vinyl ester resins for strength and adhesion, chosen for where the repair sits on the boat.
  4. 4Vacuum bag or infuse when needed. For full fiber saturation and a dense, void-free laminate on the jobs that call for it.
  5. 5Fair the surface. Professional epoxy fillers, shaped and sanded until the repair follows the boat’s original lines.
  6. 6Spray the finish. Gelcoat or marine polyurethane, color-matched to your hull.
  7. 7Wet sand, long-board and buff. Finished with AQUABUFF compounds so the repair disappears into the surrounding gelcoat.
