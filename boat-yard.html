Yard or Dockside?

The Right Place for the Right Job.

Not every repair belongs in the same place. Major structural work needs tools and equipment that can’t be set up on a dock, so those jobs come to the yard. Quick fixes and detailing are better done where the boat already is, so our service vans go to you. Our shop, yard and mobile crews work together, so whatever your boat needs, it gets done in the right place.

Can’t get the boat to us? Pick-up and transport is available — we can collect the vessel and bring it back when the work is done. Ask about it when you request a quote.

At the Yard

Big Jobs & Long Projects

Work that needs the boat hauled, blocked and opened up, with the tools and the time to do it properly.

  • Transom, stringer and bulkhead replacement
  • Hull and bottom repairs below the waterline
  • Blister and osmosis work that needs drying time
  • Full cosmetic and gelcoat restorations
  • Custom fabrication and major rebuilds

Dockside

Quick Fixes & Detailing

  • Inspections and repair estimates
