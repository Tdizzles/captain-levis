Understanding Hull Cracks, Florida

Hull Crack Repair for the Damage You Can See — and What’s Beneath It.

Fiberglass hull cracks can be cosmetic or structural. They’re often caused by impact, repeated flexing, stress, previous repairs, water intrusion or laminate failure. Our team inspects the entire area to find the root cause and repair it correctly — not just cover it up.

Common Cracks Shouldn’t Be Ignored

Small Cracks Can Lead
to Big Problems.

A hull crack isn’t just a cosmetic issue. Water intrusion, repeated flexing and impact damage can quickly turn a minor crack into a major structural repair.

  • Water IntrusionAllows water into the laminate and core, leading to rot, delamination and long-term damage.
  • Structural WeakeningReduces the hull’s strength and can compromise safety over time.
  • DelaminationWater and movement can separate fiberglass layers, making repairs more extensive.
  • Damage SpreadsCracks rarely stay the same size. Repeated stress and flexing can cause the damage to grow.

When Is a Hull Crack Cosmetic — and When Is It Structural?

Cosmetic Crack

  • Gelcoat-only surface crack
  • Shallow and does not extend into the laminate
  • No movement or soft spots
  • Typically caused by age, UV exposure or minor impact
  • Can be repaired with gelcoat and light sanding

Structural Crack

  • Extends through the gelcoat and into the laminate
  • May have soft or weak areas
  • Often caused by impact, grounding or significant stress
  • Can lead to water intrusion and delamination
  • Requires fiberglass and structural laminate repair

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?
We’ve Got Answers.

Can you repair cracks in a fiberglass boat hull?

Yes. We repair everything from hairline gelcoat cracks to cracks that run through the laminate. Structural cracks are ground back to sound fiberglass, re-laminated with marine-grade cloth and resin, then faired and gelcoated.

How do I know if a hull crack is structural?

Cracks that flex when pressed, feel soft around the edges, leak, or follow a stress line such as a chine, keel or bulkhead are usually structural. The only sure way is to inspect and sound the area — we do that before quoting.

Can you repair spider cracks?

Yes. Spider cracks are usually in the gelcoat, but we check for the cause — often flex or an old impact — so the cracks don’t come straight back after the repair.

Will the repair be color matched?

Yes. Every repair is finished with gelcoat tinted to your hull, then wet sanded and polished so it blends with the surrounding finish.

Can you repair hull cracks at my dock?

Many can. Our mobile service handles most gelcoat and smaller laminate repairs at your dock or lift. Larger structural work is best done at our Largo yard.

How long does fiberglass hull crack repair take?

Cosmetic crack repairs often take one to three days. Structural repairs depend on the size of the area and cure times — we give you a timeline with the quote.
